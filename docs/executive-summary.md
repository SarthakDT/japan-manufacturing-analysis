# Executive summary: benchmarking a prefecture's manufacturing sector

*For a prefectural planner. This is an illustrative use case in a portfolio project,
not commissioned work. Figures are for reference year 2019 unless stated, in million
yen of value added per worker, nominal.*

## The question

Your prefecture's manufacturing produces less value added per worker than the
national figure of **12.99**. Is that because of **which industries** it hosts, or
because **its industries trail the same industries elsewhere**? The answer changes
where you look first.

## What the data shows

- **Industry matters a great deal on average.** Nationally, value added per worker
  ranges almost sixfold across industries: petroleum & coal 34.8, leather 5.9. A
  prefecture weighted toward low-value industries starts behind.
- **But for most prefectures, performance inside industries matters more.** Split
  each prefecture's gap into an industry-mix part and a within-industry part: the
  within-industry part is the larger in 36–37 of 47 prefectures in every year from
  2016 to 2019. Among the 30 prefectures below the national figure in 2019, it is
  the larger part for 24.
- **Most prefectures trail on both counts.** 26 of 47 have both a weaker industry
  mix and weaker within-industry performance. This group includes large
  manufacturing prefectures such as Osaka, Saitama and Tokyo.
- **Size is not the same as value added per worker.** Aichi produces 12.8% of
  national manufacturing value added and ranks 7th. Yamaguchi, much smaller,
  ranks 1st, on the strength of chemicals.
- **Nothing is changing fast.** National value added per worker grew +0.34% a year
  in nominal terms over 2016–2019, and was flat in 2020.

## The diagnostic

| Your prefecture's position | Count | Where to look first |
|---|---|---|
| Strong mix, strong performance | 13 | Protect what works |
| Weak mix, strong performance | 5 | The industry base |
| Strong mix, weak performance | 3 | Performance inside the industries you have |
| Weak mix, weak performance | 26 | Both, starting with the larger of the two terms |

The dashboard's **Prefecture benchmark** page shows your own figures as a waterfall:
national benchmark, then industry mix, then within-industry performance, ending at
your published figure. It also lists the industries in your prefecture that beat or
miss what your region and industry would predict. Those are candidates for a closer
look.

## What this analysis cannot tell you

- **Why.** Everything here is descriptive. No cause is identified.
- **What to do.** "Within-industry performance" is not the same as firm efficiency. A
  2-digit industry such as chemicals spans very different products, so an advantage
  may simply mean making higher-value products within the same industry. It also
  reflects plant scale and the age of the equipment.
- **Per hour.** Hours worked are not published at this level, so a prefecture with
  more part-time work looks less productive per worker than it is per hour.
- **Real growth.** Values are nominal. Price changes are not removed.
- **Anything after 2020.** The source survey was abolished. Its successor publishes
  no prefecture breakdown, so this series cannot be extended.
